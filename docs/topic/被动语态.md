# 被动语态

[语法页](/grammar)讲被动的构成，本页专管「这句子该不该用被动、写出来的被动对不对」。它是完形与翻译的每年必考点：翻译技巧里的「被动→主动」一条，前提就是先认出英语里的被动。

## 判定两问

1. **动作承受者是不是更值得当主语？** 承受者是话题中心、施动者不明或不重要（by 短语说不说都行）→ 被动。
2. **动词及物吗？** 没有宾语就没有东西能搬到主语位，被动无从谈起。

## 四组核心形态

| 时态 | 被动形 | 点读 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 一般 | am/is/are + done | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The office is cleaned every day." text="The office is cleaned every day." /> |
| 进行 | am/is/are being + done | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The road is being repaired." text="The road is being repaired.（正在修）" /> |
| 完成 | has/have been + done | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The report has been finished." text="The report has been finished.（已完成）" /> |
| 情态 | 情态动词 + be + done | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The work must be done by Friday." text="The work must be done by Friday." /> |

过去时 was/were done、过去完成 had been done 同一条生产线，换助动词不换骨架。

## 这些动词变不出被动

不及物动词没有宾语（happen、occur、rise）；表所属拥有的 have、hold（容纳）、fit、suit、lack 习惯上不用被动；宾语是反身代词或相互代词时也不行：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="A car accident happened here last night." text="A car accident happened here last night.（happen 不及物，无被动）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="This jacket fits me well." text="This jacket fits me well.（不说 I am fitted by…）" />

## 短语动词变被动：介词不许丢

take care of、look into、put off 这类短语动词变被动时，动词和介词是一个整体，介词必须跟着搬：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The patients were taken good care of." text="The patients were taken good care of.（of 收尾才完整）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The case is being looked into." text="The case is being looked into.（into 不丢）" />

完形与改错里最爱在介词上设空：见到被动句尾孤零零一个介词，先别急着判错。

## get 被动：意外与经历

be 被动是常态陈述，get 被动多带「遭遇、意外」的意味，且不能带 by 短语：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="He got hurt in the match." text="He got hurt in the match.（受了伤，意外色彩）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="She gets paid every Friday." text="She gets paid every Friday.（发工资，口语常用）" />

## 被动结构还是系表结构：看状态还是动作

be + 过去分词有两种读法：表动作（被动语态）与表状态（系表结构）。gone、finished、interested、excited 这类已是纯形容词，没有对应的「动作被动」：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="I am interested in painting." text="I am interested in painting.（状态：感兴趣，非「被使感兴趣」的动作）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="All the leaves are gone." text="All the leaves are gone.（状态：没了）" />

判断法：能接 by 短语、能变进行时的是被动语态；两者都不能的是系表。

## 还有一条常考规则：「据说」句型的两次变身

It is said that he is rich（它词从句当形式主语）↔ He is said to be rich（人搬去主语位，后接不定式）。同款还有 It is believed / reported / known that…。两次变身的动词形态要咬合：It is said that he **lives**… ↔ He is said to **live**…（一般现在对应 to do）；It is said that he **was**… ↔ He is said to **have lived**…（过去对应 to have done）。
