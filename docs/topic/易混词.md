# 易混词

词汇题在形近、音近、义近三个地方设空。本页收 14 组常考易混词，每组给核心区别与例词点读。想看更全的同义替换表去[高频词汇](/exam/高频词汇)；想看构词的来历去[词根家族](/topic/词根家族)。

## 形近容易看走眼的

| 一组 | 点读 | 区分 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| affect / effect | <SpeakButton word="affect" text="affect" /> <SpeakButton word="effect" text="effect" /> | affect 是动词「影响」；effect 常作名词「效果」（have an effect on）。effect 作动词是「促成、实现」（effect change），考得少 |
| adapt / adopt / adept | <SpeakButton word="adapt" text="adapt" /> <SpeakButton word="adopt" text="adopt" /> <SpeakButton word="adept" text="adept" /> | adapt「使适应」，adapt to；adopt「采纳；收养」；adept 是形容词「熟练的」，be adept at |
| principal / principle | <SpeakButton word="principal" text="principal" /> <SpeakButton word="principle" text="principle" /> | principal「主要的；校长」；principle「原则」，in principle「原则上」。记法：你的 principal 是你的 pal（伙伴） |
| complement / compliment | <SpeakButton word="complement" text="complement" /> <SpeakButton word="compliment" text="compliment" /> | complement「补足」，与 complete 同源；compliment「赞美、恭维」，中间的 i 联想「我喜欢」的那句话 |
| stationary / stationery | <SpeakButton word="stationary" text="stationary" /> <SpeakButton word="stationery" text="stationery" /> | stationary「静止的」；stationery「文具」。两词同出「固定」一词：stationer 是守着固定摊位的文具商，一个词留下了职业，一个留下了「不动」 |
| desert / dessert | <SpeakButton word="desert" text="desert" /> <SpeakButton word="dessert" text="dessert" /> | desert「沙漠」，作动词「抛弃」；dessert「甜点」。双 s 是甜点，好吃到要两份 |

## lie 与 lay：一对动词三个意思

| 词 | 变化 | 义 | 点读 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lie | lie – lay – lain | 躺，不及物 | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="I lay on the bed for an hour yesterday." text="Yesterday I lay on the bed.（躺，过去式是 lay）" /> |
| lie | lie – lied – lied | 撒谎，规则变化 | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="He lied to me about the money." text="He lied to me.（撒谎，规则变化）" /> |
| lay | lay – laid – laid | 放置，及物 | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="She laid the baby on the bed." text="She laid the baby on the bed.（放置，及物）" /> |

易错点在「躺」的过去式恰好是 lay：**The book is lying on the desk**（正躺着）对，**The book is laying on the desk** 错。

## 自查法

14 组从头扫一遍，混的组抄进错题本，隔天回来把例词再点一遍听音，能听出区别才算过。
