# 比较结构

比较结构是阅读与词汇里的高频考点：难题不在认得 more 和 than，而在「几种比较放一起选哪个」「比较对象有没有对齐」「no more than 这种否定式陷阱认不认得」。本页按原级、比较级、最高级、否定式陷阱四条线过一遍，句式与真题例句对照见[高频语法与翻译](/exam/高频语法与翻译)。

## 原级：as…as 一族

肯定用 as…as，否定用 not as/so…as，倍数放在最前面：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="She is as busy as a bee." text="She is as busy as a bee.（原级等比）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="This room is not so large as that one." text="This room is not so large as that one.（否定原级，so 多用于否定）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="This bridge is twice as long as that one." text="This bridge is twice as long as that one.（倍数放 as 前：twice / three times）" />

## 比较级：than 前后要对齐

比较级 + than，铁律是**比较对象必须同类可比**。 population 不能和 a country 比，要和 that of a country 比：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The population of China is larger than that of India." text="The population of China is larger than that of India.（that 代替 the population，对象对齐）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The roads here are much quieter than those in my hometown." text="The roads here are much quieter than those in my hometown.（those 代替 the roads）" />

「越来越」有两种说法：比较级重叠（better and better），或 more and more + 原级：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="English is becoming more and more important." text="English is becoming more and more important.（more and more 后接原级，不说 more and more important than）" />

「越……越……」用 the + 比较级成对出现：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The more you practice, the better you get." text="The more you practice, the better you get.（两个 the 各带一个比较级）" />

## 比较级的修饰语：much 行，very 不行

比较级前只能接 much、far、a little、even、still、a lot 这类表程度的词，**very 不能直接修饰比较级**：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="This method is far more effective." text="This method is far more effective.（far 修饰比较级）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="She looks much better today." text="She looks much better today.（不说 very better）" />

要表达「好得多」且不想用比较级，可换 absolutely fantastic 这类不可分级的原级词，或改用 much better。

## no more than vs not more than：一组经典陷阱

| 句式 | 意思 | 点读 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| no more than | 只有、仅仅（嫌少） | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="He has no more than five dollars." text="He has no more than five dollars.（只有五块，言下之意不够）" /> |
| not more than | 至多、不超过（中性上限） | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="He has not more than five dollars." text="He has not more than five dollars.（不超过五块，客观陈述）" /> |

同理 no less than「多达」带惊叹，not less than「不少于」是下限。这一组在词汇题与阅读选项里都设过坑：看到 no 先问「是嫌少，还是中性封顶」。

## 最高级：范围必须圈出来

最高级要配 in / of / among 引出的范围，范围是人称代词时用 of：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="It is the largest museum in the country." text="It is the largest museum in the country.（in + 地点范围）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="She is the brightest of all the students." text="She is the brightest of all the students.（of + 一群人）" />

最高级前加 the 的一般规律有例外：副词最高级的 the 可省（He runs fastest），most 修饰原级表「非常」时不加 the（a most interesting book）。

## 还有一条常考规则：别比不可比的东西

China is larger than any country in Asia 是错句——China 自己也在 Asia 里，成了自己比自己。改用 any other country：主体在范围内要比，必须 other 把自己摘出去；主体在范围外才直接用 any：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="China is larger than any other country in Asia." text="China is larger than any other country in Asia.（自身在范围内，加 other）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Canada is larger than any country in Asia." text="Canada is larger than any country in Asia.（加拿大不在亚洲，直接 any）" />
