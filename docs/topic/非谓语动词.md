# 非谓语动词

[语法页](/grammar)的「非谓语三件套」讲各自规则，本页专管「doing、done、to do 放进同一句话里选哪个」。判定工具两件：看变形动词与逻辑主语的主被动关系，看它与谓语动词的先后关系。

## 判定三问

1. 动作是谁做的？逻辑主语主动发出 → doing；被动承受 → done。
2. 动作在谓语之前、之后还是同时？目的、将然 → to do；与谓语同时 → doing；先于谓语且强调已完成 → having done。
3. 它站在句里哪个位置？宾语位置跟前面动词的搭配惯性走（enjoy doing / decide to do），介词后面一律 doing。

## doing vs done：主动与被动

分词作状语或定语，先问「谁做的」：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Entering the room, he found everyone gone." text="Entering the room, he found everyone gone.（he 主动「进」，用 doing）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Built in 1990, the bridge is still in use." text="Built in 1990, the bridge is still in use.（桥是被建的，用 done）" />

作定语同理：the man **standing** at the gate（正站着，主动）、the language **spoken** in this area（被说，被动）。动作先于谓语且强调已完成，用 having done：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Having finished the report, she went home." text="Having finished the report, she went home.（先写完，再回家）" />

## to do vs doing 作宾语：跟前面动词的惯性走

只认 doing 的常客：enjoy、finish、practice、avoid、mind、suggest——<SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Would you mind opening the window?" text="Would you mind opening the window?" />

只认 to do 的常客：decide、hope、refuse、agree、plan——<SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="They decided to postpone the meeting." text="They decided to postpone the meeting." />

意义随形式变的是固定戏码：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="He stopped to smoke." text="He stopped to smoke.（停下来，为了去抽烟）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="He stopped smoking." text="He stopped smoking.（不再抽了）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Remember to close the window." text="Remember to close the window.（还没关，记得去关）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="I remember closing the window." text="I remember closing the window.（记得关过了）" />

forget / regret / try 同理：forget to do 是忘了去做，forget doing 是忘了做过；try to do 是努力做成，try doing 是试试看效果。

## 介词后面一律 doing

介词的宾语位置只收动名词，不管语义多像「该去做」：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="He is good at repairing bikes." text="He is good at repairing bikes.（at 后接 repairing）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Thank you for helping me with the boxes." text="Thank you for helping me with the boxes.（for 后接 helping）" />

惯用语按介词记：be interested in doing、look forward to doing、be used to doing（这里 to 是介词，区别于 used to do「过去常常」）。

## 分词作状语：逻辑主语必须认主句的主语

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Walking in the street, I met an old friend." text="Walking in the street, I met an old friend.（我在走，主语认得上，对）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Walking in the street, a car hit him." text="Walking in the street, a car hit him.（车没在走，悬垂分词，错）" />

悬垂分词的改法两条：补出主语（While he was walking in the street, a car hit him），或把分词挂回真正的主语名下。主语确实不便出席时用独立主格兜底：Weather permitting, we will go hiking（weather 挂自己名下）。

## 还有一条常考规则：感官与使役动词

see / watch / hear 带宾语时，接 do 是全过程，接 doing 是正在发生，接 done 是被动：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="I saw him cross the street." text="I saw him cross the street.（全过程：走过去了）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="I saw him crossing the street." text="I saw him crossing the street.（正在过马路）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="I saw him taken away by the police." text="I saw him taken away by the police.（他被带走，被动）" />

make / let / have 后省 to：They made him work all night。一旦变被动，to 要回来：He was made **to** work all night。
