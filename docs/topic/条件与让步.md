# 条件与让步

[语法页](/grammar)的状语从句讲结构与位置，本页专管条件与让步两族「口子上填哪个词、搭配不能犯什么忌」。它们是完形连接词空与翻译条件句的常客，也是中文习惯迁移翻车最多的地方。

## 条件家族：if 之外的五个口子

if 之外，条件口子各有性格：

| 连接词 | 意味 | 点读 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| unless | 除非（= if…not） | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="You will fail unless you work harder." text="You will fail unless you work harder.（不更努力就挂）" /> |
| as/so long as | 只要 | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="You can go out as long as you are back by ten." text="You can go out as long as you are back by ten." /> |
| provided (that) | 假如、以……为条件 | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Provided that it doesn't rain, we'll go hiking." text="Provided that it doesn't rain, we'll go hiking." /> |
| in case | 万一、以防 | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Take an umbrella in case it rains." text="Take an umbrella in case it rains.（先行防备，与 if 的条件感不同）" /> |

unless 本身含否定，句子里不要再加 not：unless you don't work 是双否定翻车。in case 后接一般现在时表将来，不接 will。

## 祈使 + and/or：藏在标点里的条件

「祈使句，+ and/or + 将来句」是条件句的变体，and 表「就」，or 表「否则」：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Study hard, and you will pass the exam." text="Study hard, and you will pass the exam.（= If you study hard, …）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Hurry up, or we will miss the train." text="Hurry up, or we will miss the train.（否则赶不上）" />

完形里见到「祈使句 + 空格 + 将来句」，空里多半就是 and 或 or。

## 让步家族：although 的两条铁律

although / though 引出让步，「虽然」不与 but 连用——中文「虽然……但是……」的直接迁移是最高频错误：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Although it was raining, we went out." text="Although it was raining, we went out.（有 although 就没有 but）" />

though 更灵活：可作副词放句尾（It was raining; we went out, though），可参与倒装（形容词提前）；although 只能当连词：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Poor though he was, he never complained." text="Poor though he was, he never complained.（倒装让步，although 做不到）" />

## even if vs even though：假设与事实一字之差

even if 是假设「就算」（还没发生），even though 是事实「尽管」（已经发生）：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Even if it rains, we will go." text="Even if it rains, we will go.（万一下，也要去——尚未发生）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Even though it rained, we went." text="Even though it rained, we went.（尽管下了——已经发生）" />

## despite 与 in spite of：介词不接从句

despite / in spite of 是介词，后面只收名词或动名词，**不接从句**；要接从句得换 although / the fact that：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Despite the heavy traffic, we arrived on time." text="Despite the heavy traffic, we arrived on time.（despite + 名词）" />
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Despite feeling tired, she kept working." text="Despite feeling tired, she kept working.（despite + 动名词）" />

词汇题爱在 although / despite / in spite of / because of 的词性上设坑：空后是句子选连词，空后是名词选介词。

## while 的三张面孔

while 在完形里出镜率极高，三种意思靠位置判：

- 当……时：<SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="While I was cooking, the phone rang." text="While I was cooking, the phone rang.（进行中的时间背景）" />
- 尽管（句首）：<SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="While I understand your point, I can't agree." text="While I understand your point, I can't agree.（让步）" />
- 而（对比）：<SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="I like tea, while my sister prefers coffee." text="I like tea, while my sister prefers coffee.（并列对比）" />

## 还有一条常考规则：条件从句里的将来不穿 will 外衣

时间与条件从句用一般现在时代替将来（主将从现），判定细则见[时态对比](/topic/时态对比)。虚拟条件（与事实相反的假设）走另一条线：时态往回退一步，规则见[语法页·语气与虚拟](/grammar#语气与虚拟)。
