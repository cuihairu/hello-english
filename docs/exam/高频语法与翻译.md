## 二、高频翻译句式结构

### 1. 条件/让步状语从句

| 句式 | 真题例句 | 年份 |
|------|---------|------|
| provided (that)... | provided work is not excessive in amount | 2015 |
| whether...or... | whether you're at a local park or camping | 2025 |
| even 引导让步（短语） | even by accident, could be harmful | 2025 |
| if 引导条件从句 | if you don't feel happy, pretend to be! | 2013 |

### 2. 定语从句与 as 从句（最高频）

| 句式 | 真题例句 | 年份 |
|------|---------|------|
| that 引导限制性 | expenses that we imagine can be cut out | 2017 |
| that 引导限制性 | a static thing that you're stuck with | 2020 |
| that 省略的限制性 | anything (that) you leave behind | 2025 |
| who 引导限制性 | ones who can have innovative ideas | 2022 |
| in which 引导非限制性 | studies, in which researchers survey the personalities | 2020 |
| as 引导非限制性定语从句 | as is the case with all friendships | 2023 |
| as 引导方式状语从句 | leaving an area exactly as you found it | 2025 |

### 3. 被动语态

| 句式 | 真题例句 | 年份 |
|------|---------|------|
| should be + p.p. | Should work be placed among the cases of happiness | 2015 |
| be regarded as | be regarded as a burden | 2015 |
| are filled with | budgets are filled with expenses | 2017 |
| be realized as | once it is realized as a duty and established as a habit | 2013 |
| can be + p.p. | change can be nearly impossible to be perceived | 2020 |

### 4. 比较结构

| 句式 | 真题例句 | 年份 |
|------|---------|------|
| less...than... | less harmful than idleness | 2015 |
| more...than... | We are more knowledgeable than people | 2021 |
| as...as 同级比较 | create as many jobs as it destroys | 2014 |
| 比较级叠用 | wider and wider circles of good will | 2013 |
| the + 比较级, the + 比较级 | （阅读中高频出现） | 多年 |

### 5. 名词性从句

| 句式 | 真题例句 | 年份 |
|------|---------|------|
| whether 引导主语从句（it 形式主语） | whether the deletion was accidental or deliberate | 2017 词汇 |
| what 引导宾语从句 | removes the need to decide what one should do | 2015 |
| that 引导表语从句 | What's sad is that even if it's the latter, we often accept it anyway | 2019 |
| that 引导宾语从句 | you will find that instead of pushing people away, you attract them | 2013 |

### 6. 非谓语动词

| 句式 | 真题例句 | 年份 |
|------|---------|------|
| 分词短语作状语 | leaving an area exactly as you found it | 2025 |
| 动名词作宾语 | practice the principles of "leave no trace" | 2025 |
| 不定式作目的状语 | To reduce your impact on nature | 2025 |
| 过去分词作后置定语 | knowledge passed on from one generation | 2021 |
| 动名词短语作主语 | Using a wide variety of brainstorming strategies | 2022 |

## 四、翻译技巧总结

### 1. 长句处理：拆分法

英语原文常用长句（一个句子包含多个从句），翻译时建议**拆分为多个短句**：

> **原文：** Our household budgets are filled with both big and small expenses that we imagine can be cut out to save us loads of cash or, at the very least, spent better elsewhere.
>
> **译文：** 我们的家庭预算中充满了大大小小的开支。我们想象这些开支可以削减，从而省下一大笔钱，或者至少可以把钱花在其他更好的地方。

### 2. 被动→主动

英语多用被动，中文多用主动：

> **原文：** work be placed among the cases of happiness
>
> **译文：** 把工作列入幸福之事

### 3. 抽象名词具体化

> **原文：** the principles of "leave no trace"
>
> **译文：** "不留痕迹"的原则

### 4. 增词与减词

- **增词**：补充中文必要的连接词和量词
- **减词**：省略英文中重复或冗余的代词

### 5. 高频翻译词汇

| 英文 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| tend to | 往往/倾向于 |
| when it comes to | 当谈到/涉及 |
| rather than | 而不是 |
| as well as | 以及/和 |
| in other words | 换言之 |
| for instance | 例如 |
| as a result | 因此 |
| in addition | 此外 |
| on the contrary | 相反 |
| to some extent | 在某种程度上 |

## 五、备考建议

1. **重点掌握定语从句翻译**：区分限制性与非限制性，注意 that/which/as 的译法
2. **非谓语动词是难点**：分词短语、动名词在翻译中常出现，需准确理解其逻辑关系
3. **关注主题趋势**：哲理、社会、学术类并列最多（各 3 次），宜全面覆盖各类话题
4. **练习段落翻译**：不要只翻单句，注意上下文衔接和整体通顺
5. **积累话题词汇**：环境保护、社会发展、教育文化等主题的高频词汇
