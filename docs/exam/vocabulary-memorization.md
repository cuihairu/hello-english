# Vocabulary quick-recall

> Use case: a skim in the 10 minutes before the exam; grab the high-frequency synonym replacements and fixed phrases first.
>
> The full version (the 148-item table, repeated high-frequency words, semantic-field grouping) is in [High-frequency vocabulary](/exam/vocabulary).

## 1. Core regularities of vocabulary questions

- Vocabulary questions are essentially synonym-replacement questions
- Verbs, adjectives and phrases are the most tested
- Not just the meaning of a word, but fixed collocations and context
- The share of "phrase replacement" is steady in the real papers and must not be ignored

## 4. High-frequency semantic groups

This part is the most worth memorizing, because the questions often swap the word without swapping the meaning.

### 1. Stop / cancel

- cease -> stop
- suspend -> halt
- call off -> cancel
- wear off -> disappear

### 2. Enough / qualification

- sufficient -> enough
- ample -> enough
- eligible -> qualified

### 3. Explain / show

- demonstrate -> show
- elaborate -> explain
- put across -> explain

### 4. Cause and effect / attribution

- give rise to -> cause
- attribute to -> cause
- consist in -> lie in

### 5. Attitude / leaning

- deliberate -> intentional
- on purpose -> deliberately
- inclined -> leaning
- reluctantly -> unwillingly

## 6. The points most easily confused

### 1. consist in / consist of

- consist in: to consist in; to lie in
- consist of: to be composed of

### 2. give out

- The real papers often test the sense of "to leak"
- Do not only memorize "to hand out"

### 3. eligible

- Often used with `for`
- Meaning "qualified"

### 4. deliberate / deliberately

- deliberate: deliberate; intentional
- deliberately / on purpose: deliberately; on purpose

### 5. critical

- In the real papers it is often not "critical; disapproving"
- More often tested as "critical; crucial" = crucial
