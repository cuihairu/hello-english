### 1. Conditional and concessive adverbial clauses

| Pattern | Example from the papers | Year |
|------|---------|------|
| provided (that)... | provided work is not excessive in amount | 2015 |
| whether...or... | whether you're at a local park or camping | 2025 |
| even introducing concession (phrase) | even by accident, could be harmful | 2025 |
| if introducing a condition clause | if you don't feel happy, pretend to be! | 2013 |

### 2. Relative clauses and as clauses (the most frequent)

| Pattern | Example from the papers | Year |
|------|---------|------|
| that, restrictive | expenses that we imagine can be cut out | 2017 |

## 4. Translation techniques

### 1. Long sentences: the splitting method

The English originals often use long sentences (one sentence holding several clauses); when translating, it is best to **split them into several short sentences**:

> **Original:** Our household budgets are filled with both big and small expenses that we imagine can be cut out to save us loads of cash or, at the very least, spent better elsewhere.
>
> **Translation:** 我们的家庭预算中充满了大大小小的开支。我们想象这些开支可以削减，从而省下一大笔钱，或者至少可以把钱花在其他更好的地方。

### 2. Passive to active

English favors the passive and Chinese the active:

> **Original:** work be placed among the cases of happiness
>
> **Translation:** 把工作列入幸福之事

### 3. Making abstract nouns concrete

> **Original:** the principles of "leave no trace"
>
> **Translation:** "不留痕迹"的原则

### 4. Adding and omitting words

- **Adding**: supply the connectives and measure words that Chinese needs
- **Omitting**: drop pronouns that are repeated or redundant in the English

## 5. Preparation record

1. **Master the translation of relative clauses**: tell restrictive from non-restrictive, and mind how that/which/as are rendered
2. **Non-finite verbs are the hard part**: participial phrases and gerunds appear constantly in translation, and their logical relations must be read exactly
3. **Watch the topic trend**: philosophy, society and academic topics tie for most (3 each), so it is best to cover every category of topic
4. **Practice whole passages**: do not translate single sentences only — mind the cohesion between sentences and the overall smoothness
5. **Collect topic vocabulary**: the high-frequency words of environment, social development, education and culture topics
