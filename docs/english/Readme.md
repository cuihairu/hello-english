# Hello English

Hello English collects my English notes, topic observations, and exam-preparation materials, and also records the development process of turning those materials into a searchable, tappable site. The site is built with VitePress:

- **Learning notes**: [Pronunciation](/pronunciation) (phonemes and tap-to-hear), [Word roots](/roots), [History](/history), [History timeline](/timeline), [Grammar](/grammar), [Tense](/tense) — recording sound, word formation, evolution, and sentence rules.
- **Topics**: [Confusing words](/topic/confusing-words), [Prepositions](/topic/prepositions), [Root families](/topic/root-families), [Long and complex sentences](/topic/long-sentences), [Tense comparison](/topic/tense-comparison), [Pronunciation hard spots](/topic/pronunciation-hard-parts), [Non-finite verbs](/topic/non-finite-verbs), [Comparison structures](/topic/comparison-structures), [Clause connectors](/topic/clause-connectors), [Passive voice](/topic/passive-voice), [Condition and concession](/topic/condition-and-concession) — one class of problem gathered into one page.
- **Exam materials**: [Key-point analysis](/exam/key-points) and [past papers](/exam/papers/sources), with key-point statistics across six dimensions (vocabulary, oral communication, grammar and translation, writing, reading, cloze and passage completion), plus 16 assembled past papers from 2010 to 2025 (new-format years from 2013 and old-format years 2010-2012 collected separately).

## Exam structure

(Explanatory note for the papers used in the past-paper section.) The national unified exam is worth 100 points and lasts 150 minutes, spread over two papers:

| Paper | Section | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper 1 | Oral communication | 10 |
| Paper 1 | Vocabulary | 10 |
| Paper 1 | Reading comprehension | 25 |
| Paper 1 | Cloze | 10 |
| Paper 1 | Passage completion | 20 |
| Paper 2 | Translation (English into Chinese) | 10 |
| Paper 2 | Writing | 15 |

> The paper structure above is the format after the 2013 question-type reform; 2010-2012 used the old format (Paper 1 with oral communication, vocabulary, reading, and cloze, Paper 2 with translation and writing, translation worth 20 points) and is kept only for comparison.

## How the content is organized

The systematic notes hold my organization of pronunciation, roots, grammar, and tense; the topic pages record specific language phenomena; the exam pages hold past-paper materials, question-type statistics, and personal exam-preparation experience. The pages link to one another, so browse as needed.

## Content notes

- The past-paper pages were assembled from public recollections and PDF transcriptions and are **not official editions**.
- This site uses uniform markers for uncertain content: **[to be supplied]** (content missing), **to be proofread** (translations etc. compiled by this site), **to be verified** (answers derived from context, not checked against an official source).
- During assembly, transcription errors were corrected and derivable reference answers were completed, but some damaged content (such as the English question text of 2022 Reading Section B and the 2024 writing model essay) truly has no source to consult, so the markers are kept; the reference translations and model essays not supplied for Paper 2 (2015, 2018, 2021, 2023) are likewise marked **[to be supplied]**; answers derived from context carry a separate **to be verified** marker.
