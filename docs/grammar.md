# 语法

语法回答一个问题：**词怎么排成句，句怎么接成段**。本页先给一张体系地图，再逐块讲每块的核心逻辑与最易错点；时态单独立了一页（见[时态](/tense)），考试侧重的统计与长难句拆解在[高频语法与翻译](/exam/高频语法与翻译)。例句均可点读出声。

## 体系地图

```
英语语法
├── 词法：词的岗位
│   ├── 八大词类 + 冠词
│   └── 动词专题：时态 · 语态 · 语气 · 非谓语 · 情态动词
└── 句法：岗位的组合
    ├── 五大基本句型（骨架）
    ├── 三大从句（骨架的叠加：名词性 · 定语 · 状语）
    └── 倒装 · 强调 · 省略（骨架的变形）
```

读长难句的顺序就是这张地图自上而下：先找谓语动词定主句骨架，再看从句挂在哪个位置，最后处理倒装省略这些变形。

## 八大词类与冠词

| 词类 | 岗位 | 例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 名词 n. | 人、物、概念的名字 | city，honesty |
| 动词 v. | 动作与状态，句子的心脏 | run，seem |
| 形容词 adj. | 修饰名词 | tall，important |
| 副词 adv. | 修饰动词、形容词、整句 | quickly，however |
| 代词 pron. | 顶替名词避免重复 | he，which |
| 介词 prep. | 挂接名词与其它成分 | in，despite |
| 连词 conj. | 连接词与句 | and，although |
| 感叹词 int. | 独立于句子结构之外 | oh，well |

冠词单列一组，三条核心：**a/an 表泛指某一个**（首次提及、任取一个）；**the 表特指**（双方已知、上文提过、或独一无二——the sun）；**零冠词**用于泛指的复数与不可数名词（Books are cheap.）。最易错的一处：泛指的可数名词单数必须带冠词，不能光杆——说 I like dog 是「我喜欢吃狗肉」，说「我喜欢狗」得是 I like dogs 或 the dog。

## 五大基本句型

所有英语句子都是五种骨架之一（或其扩展）。判断句型只看一件事：**谓语动词后面跟着谁**。

| 句型 | 公式 | 例句 | 识别要点 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主谓 SV | S + V | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Birds fly." text="Birds fly." /> | 不及物动词自己收尾 |
| 主系表 SVC | S + V + C | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The soup tastes good." text="The soup tastes good." /> | 系动词（be/seem/taste…）后接的是**补语**，说明主语 |
| 主谓宾 SVO | S + V + O | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="She teaches English." text="She teaches English." /> | 及物动词带一个宾语 |
| 主谓双宾 SVOO | S + V + O(间) + O(直) | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="He gave me a book." text="He gave me a book." /> | give/tell/show/send 一族带人+物两个宾语 |
| 主谓宾补 SVOC | S + V + O + C | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="They call him a fool." text="They call him a fool." /> | 宾语后还有一块补语说明宾语 |

**双宾与宾补的区别用 be 检验**：He gave me a book 里 me ≠ a book（不能说 I am a book），是双宾；They call him a fool 里 him = a fool（him is a fool 成立），a fool 是宾补。一个 be 动词分清两个句型。

## 三大从句

从句是「句中句」，按它在大句里顶什么岗分三类：顶名词岗是名词性从句，顶形容词岗是定语从句，顶副词岗是状语从句。

### 名词性从句

that / whether / wh- 词引导的句子，可作主语、宾语、表语、同位语：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="What he said surprised everyone." text="What he said surprised everyone." />（主语）
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="I know that she lives here." text="I know that she lives here." />（宾语；that 在宾语位置常省略）
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The news that he won spread fast." text="The news that he won spread fast." />（同位语，说明 news 的内容）

易错两处：**语序**——从句一律陈述语序，Do you know where does he live 是错的，where he live**s** 才对；**that 与 what**——that 只起连接作用不作成分，what 自身在从句里充当主语或宾语。

### 定语从句

关系代词 who/which/that 引导，修饰名词：

- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The book that I bought yesterday is good." text="The book that I bought yesterday is good." />（that 作 bought 的宾语）
- <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="The man who lives next door is a doctor." text="The man who lives next door is a doctor." />（who 作从句主语，谓语单复数跟着先行词）

要点四条：**只用 that** 的情形（先行词是 all/everything/不定代词、或被序数词最高级修饰）；**介词后不用 that**（in which 正确，in that 不行）；**非限制性**（逗号隔开的补充说明）用 which/who 不用 that；最易错的是**关系词必须作成分**——The book that I bought **it** is good 这种「双主语」是中式英语的常见病，it 必须删。

### 状语从句

按意义分九类（时间、地点、条件、原因、目的、结果、让步、方式、比较），引导词各有分工，先记高频四类：

| 类别 | 引导词 | 例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 时间 | when / while / as / before / until | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="I was reading when he called." text="I was reading when he called." /> |
| 条件 | if / unless / as long as | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="If it rains, we will stay home." text="If it rains, we will stay home." /> |
| 让步 | although / though / even if | Although he is young, he is reliable. |
| 原因 | because / since / as | Since you are here, let's start. |

**主将从现**：时间与条件从句用一般现在时表将来，主句才用将来时——If it **will rain** 是错的（见[时态页](/tense)详解）。

## 非谓语三件套

动词不当谓语时要「变形」：不定式 to do、动名词 doing、分词 doing/done。三者分工的总纲：

| 形式 | 核心含义 | 例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| to do | 目的、将然 | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="He went abroad to study." text="He went abroad to study." />（去是为了学） |
| doing | 主动、进行 | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Standing on the hill, we saw the sea." text="Standing on the hill, we saw the sea." />（we 站着，主动） |
| done | 被动、完成 | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="Seen from the hill, the town looks small." text="Seen from the hill, the town looks small." />（镇子被看，被动） |

经典对比成对记：stop **to do**（停下来去做另一件事）/ stop **doing**（停止手头的事）；remember **to do**（记得要做，还没做）/ remember **doing**（记得做过）。分词的**逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致**——Walking in the street, a car hit him 是悬垂分词错误（车没在走路），改成 While he was walking, …。

## 语气与虚拟

语气三分：陈述（说事）、祈使（指令）、虚拟（说反话——与事实相反或难以实现的假设）。虚拟语气的要点是**时态往回退一步**：

| 假设类型 | 从句 | 主句 | 例 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 与现在相反 | 过去式（be 一律用 were） | would + 动原 | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="If I were you, I would accept it." text="If I were you, I would accept it." /> |
| 与过去相反 | had done | would have done | <SpeakButton kind="sentence" word="If he had studied, he would have passed." text="If he had studied, he would have passed." /> |
| 与将来相反（可能小） | should / were to do | would + 动原 | If it were to snow tomorrow, we would cancel it. |

wish 后的从句同样回退：I wish I **knew**（现在）/ I wish I **had known**（过去）。另一组高频考点是 suggest/insist/order/demand 等「要求类」动词的宾语从句用 (should) + 动词原形——He suggested that she **(should) leave** early。注意 suggest 表「建议」才虚拟，表「表明」不虚拟（The smile suggests that he **is** happy）。

## 被动语态

被动 = **be 的一套时态形式 + 过去分词**，动作的承受者当主语。常用八格（与[时态页](/tense)的 16 格矩阵对应）：

| 时态 | 被动形式 | 例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 一般现在 | am/is/are done | English is spoken here. |
| 现在进行 | am/is/are being done | The road is being repaired. |
| 一般过去 | was/were done | The bridge was built in 1900. |
| 过去进行 | was/were being done | The house was being painted. |
| 现在完成 | has/have been done | The work has been finished. |
| 过去完成 | had been done | The letter had been sent. |
| 一般将来 | will be done | A new school will be opened. |
| 情态动词 | can/must be done | The rule must be followed. |

两个习惯提醒：不及物动词没有被动（happen/take place 不说 was happened）；get + done 是口语里的被动变体（He got hired last week），多表遭遇或结果。

## 情态动词

推测的确定性有一条梯度：**must（一定）> may（可能）> might（可能性更小）**；否定推测用 can't（不可能），不是 mustn't——He must be tired 是「一定累」，He can't be tired 才是「不可能累」。情态动词 + have done 表对过去的推测：must **have done**（过去一定做了）、should **have done**（本该做没做，带责备）、needn't **have done**（本不必做却做了）。

## 倒装、强调与省略

- **部分倒装**：否定词开头的状语提前，助动词提到主语前——Never **have I seen** such a mess. / Not only **does he** sing, but he also dances. / Only then **did I realize** the truth.
- **so/neither 倒装**：So do I（我也是）/ Neither do I（我也不），助动词与前面句子的时态一致。
- **强调句**：It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余。检验法：去掉 It is…that 后句子仍完整就是强调句——It was in Paris **that** they met → In Paris they met ✓。
- **省略**：比较状语从句里与主句相同的成分可省——She is taller than I (am). / He runs faster than she (does)。省到只剩从属连词的状语从句（When asked, he nodded = When he was asked）是书面语的常态。
